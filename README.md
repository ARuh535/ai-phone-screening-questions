# AI Phone Screening Questions: Templates by Role

Free, ready-to-use screening questions for first-round calls, whether a recruiter or an AI phone screening agent asks them.

**Rule of thumb:** 3–5 must-have questions per role, each answerable in under a minute.

## Customer support
- Are you available for the shift pattern listed, including weekends?
- Tell me about a time you calmed down an upset customer. What did you say?
- Which support or ticketing tools have you used?

## Warehouse & logistics
- Do you have a valid forklift or equipment certification?
- Can you regularly lift up to 50 lbs?
- How will you get to the site for a 6am start?

## Retail & hospitality
- Which days and hours can you work each week?
- Describe a busy shift and how you handled it.
- When could you start?

## Healthcare support
- Which certifications do you hold, and when do they expire?
- Are you comfortable with rotating or overnight shifts?
- Tell me about a time you followed a strict safety or hygiene procedure.

## Software engineering (first round)
- Which language and framework have you used most in the last year?
- Walk me through a bug you fixed recently. How did you find the cause?
- Why did you choose that approach over the alternative?

## Fair screening checklist
- [ ] Candidates are told up front if the call is with an AI
- [ ] Every candidate for a role gets the same questions
- [ ] Answers are transcribed so reviewers can read them
- [ ] A recruiter approves every rejection
- [ ] Pass rates are checked across groups every month (see NYC Local Law 144)

## What to measure
| Metric | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Time from application to first contact | Strong candidates accept other offers fast |
| % of applicants screened | Shows who never got a first call |
| AI shortlist vs hiring manager agreement | Tells you if the questions work |
